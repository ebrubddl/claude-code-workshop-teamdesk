The feature

Three new things you can do

All of them are on the existing Tasks page. There's no new screen to learn.

1

Pick a priority when you create a task

The new-task form has a Priority select next to Assignee. If you don't touch it, the task is Medium.

2

See it, and change it, on every row

A badge next to the title shows the priority, and High stands out in red. Change it from the dropdown at any time. On completed tasks the dropdown is locked, the same as Assignee.

3

Filter by priority

Toggle Low, Medium and High above the list, and combine them as you like. With nothing selected, everything shows.

See it in action

The filter, step by step

Real screenshots from the browser check. Pick a step.

Nothing selected: every task shows, in the usual order.

High selected: only High tasks are left.

High and Low selected: both show; Medium is hidden.

Nothing matches: a short message explains why the list is empty.
